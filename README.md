# BlumTherapy 🌍

> **A second chance, grounded in evidence: recovery support, education, meaningful work, and human connection.**

BlumTherapy is the English-first, international counterpart to BlumHelps. It aims to help people affected by substance use find trustworthy treatment pathways, peer and community support, education, employment resources, healthy activities, and practical ways to rebuild their lives.

**Status:** early Android scaffold. The app does not yet provide treatment, crisis response, or a complete verified service directory. Country-specific resources must be reviewed before they are presented as current or available.

## Product vision
- Discover professional treatment and evidence-informed recovery support.
- Connect recovery goals with learning, employment, volunteering, physical activity, and community.
- Offer optional progress milestones and ethical incentives only when funding, rules, and delivery are real.
- Support families and trusted people without stigma, blame, or unrealistic promises.
- Localize content by country and region instead of treating one country's health system as universal.

## Principles
1. Human dignity is not conditional on abstinence or perfectly linear recovery.
2. The app is not a diagnostic tool, clinician, emergency service, or substitute for professional care.
3. Scientific claims require traceable evidence and plain-language limitations.
4. Faith-based support may be an opt-in choice; it must never be required.
5. No fabricated benefits, treatment slots, partners, grants, jobs, or discount codes.
6. Do not collect sensitive health or substance-use data by default.
7. Do not use shame, coercion, punitive streaks, or financial pressure to influence treatment decisions.

## International starting points
- **United States:** [FindTreatment.gov](https://www.findtreatment.gov/) — confidential, anonymous treatment locator.
- **United Kingdom:** [NHS drug addiction support](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/addiction-support/drug-addiction-getting-help/) — confirm local availability and UK nation.
- **Canada:** [Health Canada substance-use treatment](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/substance-use/treatment.html).
- **Australia:** [Australian Government Drug Help](https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/drug-help); the government lists the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline at 1800 250 015, 24/7. Reconfirm before display.
- **Global evidence:** [WHO/UNODC treatment standards](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/international-standards-for-the-treatment-of-drug-use-disorders).

These sources are starting points, not endorsements or guarantees of eligibility. Costs, insurance, crisis contacts and service availability vary by location and must be checked.
