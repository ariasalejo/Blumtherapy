# Evidence, Dignity, and Ethics

Hope can motivate change; health claims require evidence. BlumTherapy is not a diagnostic tool, clinician, emergency service, or replacement for professional care.

## Initial evidence base
1. [WHO/UNODC International Standards for the Treatment of Drug Use Disorders (2020)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/international-standards-for-the-treatment-of-drug-use-disorders): ethical, evidence-based treatment-system principles.
2. [WHO: psychosocial support for stimulant-use disorders (updated 2023)](https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/treatment-care/mental-health-gap-action-programme/evidence-centre/drug-use-disorders/psychosocial-support-for-the-management-of-psychostimulant-use-disorders): recommends offering CBT and contingency management for cocaine/stimulant dependence; evidence certainty is stated on the page.
3. [Systematic review of contingency management (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39545650/): moderate-to-large effects for single-substance interventions; do not generalize automatically to every substance, population, or informal reward scheme.
4. [Systematic review of recovery housing (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40115346/): promising outcomes for substance use, income, and employment, but overall evidence is moderate and further comparative studies are needed.
5. [Health Canada: treatment and support](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/substance-use/treatment.html): there is no one-size-fits-all treatment; returning to care after recurrence of use is valuable.

## Product rules
- Use non-stigmatizing language. Recurrence of use does not make a person a failure and must not block future support.
- Respect individual goals; clinical goals should be discussed with qualified care providers.
- When appropriate, connect clinical care with peer support, family, housing, education, employment, and meaningful activities.
- Never recommend abrupt withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines, or prescribed medication without medical assessment.
- Distinguish evidence, personal testimony, belief, and opinion.
- Faith-based support is opt-in and never a condition of access; clinical care respects all beliefs and no belief.
- Describe uncertainty, limitations, source dates, and conflicts of interest.
- Clinical content must be reviewed by qualified professionals before being presented as guidance.

Every health claim needs a record of exact wording, source, publication/update date, studied population, intervention, limitations, reviewer role, review date, and next review date. Testimonials are lived experiences, not proof of efficacy.

No guaranteed cure, rapid detox, guaranteed recovery, employment, grants, or claims that an app alone treats substance-use disorders. Do not infer clinical status from app engagement.
