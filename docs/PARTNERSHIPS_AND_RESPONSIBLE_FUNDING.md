# Partnerships, sponsorship and responsible funding

Updated: 2026-10-13

## Product boundaries
BlumTherapy is an English-first international companion to BlumHelps, which prioritizes Spanish and Colombia. They are separate products. Neither is a healthcare provider, emergency service, benefits agency, or treatment substitute.

## Potential partner categories — not confirmed sponsors
- Universities and research groups in public health, psychology, social work, human-computer interaction, accessibility and implementation science.
- Licensed treatment providers and community recovery organizations that can review local pathways and referral language.
- Education, vocational training and employment services that can publish official, verifiable opportunities.
- Employers and corporate social-impact / ESG programs funding accessibility, digital inclusion, skills training or evaluation without access to user-level health data.
- Foundations and philanthropic programs focused on mental health, substance-use recovery, employment inclusion, youth support and social reintegration.
- Public innovation and health research funders. Check each official call's current status, applicant eligibility, geography, required consortium, co-funding and deadlines before investing time.

## Funding sequence
1. In-kind pilot: professional review, accessibility testing, translation, devices, training places and community feedback.
2. Small 3–6 month evaluation with a university or community organization; agree outcomes, safeguarding, data governance and independent review in advance.
3. Scale only after evidence of usability, safe referrals, accessibility, engagement and sustainable operating costs.

## Sponsor package
Prepare a one-page theory of change; working demo and honest feature list; audience and geography; line-item budget and milestones; privacy and safeguarding policies; evaluation plan; named project owner; and a draft memorandum of understanding covering responsibilities, use of logos, funding conditions, intellectual property, reporting and exit.

## Responsible incentives
- Prefer in-kind support: transport, data access, courses, books, sports or arts activities, mentoring and job-readiness services.
- Never condition treatment or support on abstinence, drug tests, purchases, religious participation or disclosure of sensitive data.
- Do not punish relapse, missed check-ins or pauses in app use.
- Publish sponsor, funding amount or available pool, eligibility, dates, number of awards, selection process, delivery method and complaint route before announcing rewards.
- Sponsors must not influence clinical content, eligibility decisions or access to individual data.
- Do not accept advertising for alcohol, gambling, tobacco, miracle cures or unverified providers.
- Collect no substance-use, diagnosis, precise location or contact-list data by default; never sell individual profiles.

## Transparency
A directory listing, introduction or application is not a partnership or award. Do not use logos or announce a sponsor until written permission is in place. Verify each grant at the official source immediately before applying.
